Motivation

“The enjoyment of one’s tools is an essential ingredient of successful work.”

  • Donald Knuth

Let’s try something different: a persuasion-style blog post. Look, I’m sure that if you have been using a computer for a while, you probably have some setup you are comfortable with. Perhaps, it’s slow or perhaps it’s fast, but it’s yours. I’m not here to tell you that you are wrong, but I am here to tell how I created a setup that I love. One that is fast, efficient, and most importantly, mine. Believe or not the last point is extremely important: you will be spending a lot of time in front of your computer, so why not make it a place you love to be in? This is the first post in a series where I will be showing you how I use my tools and how I have optimized my workflow to be as efficient as possible. You can find the full series here: SeniorMars’s Workflow. Now, before we get any further let me show you some screenshots:

With that introduction out of the way, if you are a comfortable beginner, I’d prefer you spend your time creating and then learning. You will learn something new from this post, but you will learn more from failing and exploring. While I enjoy customizing my setup, I wish I spent more time creating when I was younger. Your setup will evolve as you do, so don’t worry about getting it perfect the first time. Finally, here are my dotfiles: SeniorMars’s Dotfiles.
